# Blyx Public Language & Ecosystem Roadmap

Official Domain: https://blyx-lang.space

---

## 1. Beta v1.0 (Current)
- Completed BIR compilation engine (`compiler/blyx_bir`), LLVM IR emitter, `blyxpkg`, `blyx-analyzer`, and documentation suite.

## 2. v1.0 Stable Release Target (Q2 2027)
- Language specification stabilization.
- Self-hosted parser frontend (`blyx_parser`).
- Distributed package registry at `https://blyx-lang.space/registry`.

## 3. v1.1 & Future Milestones
- Advanced SIMD auto-vectorization passes.
- Dynamic tensor shape inference.
- Multi-node distributed actor clustering runtime.
